Quick facts

Lake Oswego at a glance

Where it is

About 8 miles south of downtown Portland, along the Willamette River.

Who lives here

About 40,000 residents, in a city built around Oswego Lake.

Which county

Most of the city sits in Clackamas County, with pieces in Multnomah and Washington counties.

Getting around

I-5, Highway 217 and Highway 43 connect you to Portland, Beaverton, Tualatin and West Linn.

Good questions

Ask Tam

Is Lake Oswego part of Portland?

No. Lake Oswego is its own city, about 8 miles south of downtown Portland, inside the Portland Metro area.

Can I buy a home in Lake Oswego without visiting first?

Yes. I tour homes for you on live video, and most paperwork gets signed electronically. Many buyers still like one trip before closing.

Does every Lake Oswego home come with lake access?

No. Since 2025 the public is allowed to paddle and swim in from Millennium Plaza Park, but docks, power boating and easement lots go with specific addresses. See my lake access guide.

Which part of Lake Oswego fits me?

Tell me how you want to live: walkable, lakefront, wooded, quick commute or acreage. I'll match you to the right neighborhoods.

What should I do first?
