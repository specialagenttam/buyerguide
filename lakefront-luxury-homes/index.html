Lakefront 101

What comes with a lakefront home

Corporation stock

Lakefront owners get one Lake Oswego Corporation share per 10 feet of frontage and pay annual assessments.

Docks and boathouses

Building or changing a dock, boathouse or seawall takes a Corporation Facility Permit. Boathouses cap at 500 square feet with no living space.

Boat rules

Every boat needs a Corporation license. Boats max out at 22 feet, or 22 feet 6 inches for pontoons. Daytime speed tops out at 40 MPH, with 8 MPH after dark.

Inspections

Seawalls, docks, lifts and boathouses need their own inspection. I line up specialists who know the lake.

Good questions

Ask Tam

What does it cost to own a lakefront home in Lake Oswego?

Beyond the mortgage and taxes, plan for Lake Oswego Corporation assessments based on frontage, plus permits, boat licenses and dock and seawall upkeep. I'll pull the numbers for any home you like.

Do lakefront homes come with power boating?

Yes, lakefront shareholders power boat with a Corporation boat license and operator's license, under the lake's size and speed rules.

What's the difference between lakefront and lake easement?

Lakefront homes sit on the water and hold Corporation stock. Easement homes share one of 20 easement lots, pay easement and Corporation fees, and usually get one power boat slip.

Can you sell my luxury home quietly?

Yes. We'll talk through private, pre-market and public options and pick the one matching your goals.
