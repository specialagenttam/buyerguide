About this home

The story

A level acre gives you room for projects, a bigger garden or a workshop full of restoration work. The detached shop backs that up.

Inside, you get single-level living, a big kitchen made for gathering and a gas fireplace. The primary suite has its own exterior door, a walk-in closet, a soaking tub and a separate shower. The property sits in unincorporated Clackamas County, outside city limits.
