About this home

The story

This one sits on a private lot that feels tucked away, close to both Portland and Lake Oswego. You get two living spaces: a family room with a fireplace and a vaulted living room with a bay window.

The kitchen has granite counters and stainless gas appliances and opens to the family room. A slider off the breakfast nook leads to the refinished deck and fenced yard. The primary suite sits apart from the other bedrooms, and one bedroom has built-in shelves that work well as an office.
