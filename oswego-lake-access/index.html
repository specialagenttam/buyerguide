Lake Oswego, explained

How Oswego Lake
access works

The short answer: since 2025, anyone is allowed to paddle or swim in from Lower Millennium Plaza Park. Docks, power boats and private easement lots still go with specific addresses. Here's how it all fits together.

Who gets what

Three ways onto the lake

The public

Since 2025, anyone is allowed to enter from the concrete platforms at Lower Millennium Plaza Park, from an hour before sunrise to an hour after sunset. Non-motorized craft only, up to 18 feet, with a life jacket or swim buoy.

Easement members

About 3,000 Lake Oswego homes carry deeded easement rights to one of 20 Lake Oswego Corporation easement lots. Members pay their easement and the Corporation, then swim, launch and keep a boat under the rules.

Lakefront owners

Lakefront owners hold Lake Oswego Corporation stock, one share per 10 feet of frontage, and pay annual assessments. They get docks, boathouses and power boating under Corporation permits and licenses.

Before you buy

What I check on every lake-area home

Does the address carry rights?

Lake privileges come from the property's deed history. The Corporation lets you check eligibility by street number. I check it before you write an offer.

Is the home attached?

Condos, apartments, townhomes and other attached homes don't qualify for lake privileges under Corporation rules, even next to the water.

What does the easement cost?

2026 Corporation easement fees run $228 for swim and non-power use and $1,566 for a power boat, plus your easement association's own dues.

What comes with lakefront?

Assessments, dock and boathouse permits, seawall upkeep and boat licenses. I bring in the right inspectors and pull the paperwork.

Power boat plans?

Boats need a Corporation boat license, operators need a license and exam, and boats max out at 22 feet. Easement homes get one power boat slip, with a few older exceptions.

What about the appeal?

The Lake Oswego Corporation appealed the public access ruling to the Oregon Court of Appeals in 2026. Current rules stay in place while the appeal runs. I'll keep you posted.

Timeline

How we got here

2022

A Clackamas County judge ruled the lake was historically navigable, so public trust rules apply.

November 2024

Judge Kathie Steele found the city's ban on public access unreasonable.

March 2025

The court ordered the city to open access at Millennium Plaza Park. The City Council voted 5-2 not to appeal.

2026

The Lake Oswego Corporation filed its brief with the Oregon Court of Appeals. Public access rules remain in effect for now.

Good questions

Ask Tam

Is Oswego Lake public or private?

Both, in a way. Since 2025 the public is allowed to enter by paddling or swimming from Lower Millennium Plaza Park. Docks, power boating and the 20 easement lots stay with lakefront owners and easement members through the Lake Oswego Corporation.

Can I bring a motorboat on Oswego Lake?

Only if you're a lakefront shareholder or an easement member with a Corporation boat license and operator's license. The public is allowed non-motorized craft up to 18 feet.

Do condos in Lake Oswego come with lake access?

Not private lake privileges. Corporation rules exclude condos, apartments, townhomes and other attached homes. Anyone is still free to use the public entry at Millennium Plaza Park.

How do I know if a house has lake rights?

Ask me. I check the deed history and the Corporation's eligibility list by street number before you write an offer.

Where can I swim in Lake Oswego?

The public is allowed to swim in from Lower Millennium Plaza Park with a life jacket or swim buoy. Easement members swim at their easement lot. The city also runs a seasonal swim park.
