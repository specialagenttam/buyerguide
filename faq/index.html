Buying

Do I sign anything before we tour?

Yes. We sign a buyer agreement first. It spells out what I do for you and how I'm paid. I'll walk you through every line.

Do I need to be pre-approved?

Before you write an offer, yes. A pre-approval shows sellers you're serious and tells you your real budget.

How long does buying take?

Most buyers spend a few weeks to a few months searching. Once your offer is accepted, closing usually takes about a month.

Can I buy and sell at the same time?

Yes. We plan the timing together so you're not stuck with two homes or none. My Seller's Guide covers the selling side.

Do you help with lakefront homes?

Yes. I hold eXp's Luxury certification, and I'll walk you through lake rights, docks, seawalls and Lake Oswego Corporation rules.

Selling

When should I call you?

Earlier than you think. Even if you're a year out, a walk-through now tells you what's worth fixing and what to leave alone, so you don't spend money in the wrong places.

Should I buy my next home first or sell first?

It depends on your finances, your timeline and how much risk feels okay. We'll walk through both paths, and I'll connect you with a lender I trust to see what's possible.

Do I need to stage?

Not always. Sometimes it's a few key pieces, sometimes it's rearranging what you already own. If staging makes sense, I offer it myself, with my own furniture, whether your home is empty or you still live there.

Can I stay in my home while it's on the market?

Yes. Lots of my sellers do. We'll plan showings around your life, kids and pets included.

What about the online value estimates?

They're a starting point. Your updates, your street and this week's sales matter more. I'll give you a real price range with the sales behind it. Start with my home value tool.

Can you sell my luxury home quietly?

Yes. We'll talk through private, pre-market and public options and pick the one matching your goals.

Lake Oswego and the lake

Is Oswego Lake public or private?

Both, in a way. Since 2025 the public is allowed to enter by paddling or swimming from Lower Millennium Plaza Park. Docks, power boating and the 20 easement lots stay with lakefront owners and easement members through the Lake Oswego Corporation.

How do I know if a house has lake rights?

Ask me. I check the deed history and the Corporation's eligibility list by street number before you write an offer.

What's the difference between lakefront and lake easement?

Lakefront homes sit on the water and hold Corporation stock. Easement homes share one of 20 easement lots, pay easement and Corporation fees, and usually get one power boat slip. More on my Oswego Lake Access page.

Do condos in Lake Oswego come with lake access?

Not private lake privileges. Corporation rules exclude condos, apartments, townhomes and other attached homes. Anyone is still free to use the public entry at Millennium Plaza Park.

Which neighborhood fits me?

Start with my neighborhood guides, then book a tour with me. Seeing a few areas in one afternoon tells you more than any map.
