// Privacy Policy

Privacy, plain and simple.

This website is run by Tamara Stone, a real estate agent with eXp Realty, in Lake Oswego, Oregon. You can reach her at (503) 349-5236.

What this site collects

If you fill out the contact form, you send your name, email address, and anything else you choose to include, such as your phone number, buying timeline, and what you're looking for. That information goes straight to Tamara Stone by email so she can reply to you.

If you use the guide download form, you send your name, email address, and your phone number if you choose to share it. That information is used to send you the guide and to follow up with you about buying a home in Lake Oswego. If you check the texting box, you agree Tamara Stone may text you about Lake Oswego real estate.

This site does not run accounts, does not track you across the web, and does not sell your information to anyone.

Home search and home value tools

The home search, listings and home value tools on this site are provided by RealScout. If you use them to save a search, create an account or request a home value, the details you enter are shared with RealScout and with Tamara Stone so she can follow up with you. RealScout's own privacy policy applies to the information it collects.

What happens to your information

Form submissions are used only to follow up with you about buying or selling a home. You will not be added to a marketing list you didn't ask for, and you can ask to have your information deleted at any time by contacting Tamara Stone at (503) 349-5236.

Other links

This site links to Calendly for booking a call. Calendly has its own privacy policy, which applies when you use it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
