Why it matters

You only get one chance to be the new listing.

When your home hits the market, every buyer waiting for a home like yours gets the alert at the same moment. Most of the best showings and strongest offers come in the first two weeks. Everything we do before launch day is about making those two weeks count.

The heart side

It's okay if this is emotional.

You might be leaving the home where your kids grew up, or where you hosted every holiday for twenty years. Selling is nerve-wracking for anyone, even when the move is a happy one.

I've taken courses in marriage and family counseling, and I lean on that training more than you'd think. Some days my job is pricing strategy. Some days it's sitting at your kitchen table while you decide which boxes go to storage and which memories come with you. I never rush that part.

Seller questions

What sellers ask me

When should I call you?

Earlier than you think. Even if you're a year out, a walk-through now tells you what's worth fixing and what to leave alone, so you don't spend money in the wrong places.

Should I buy my next home first or sell first?

It depends on your finances, your timeline and how much risk feels okay. We'll walk through both paths, and I'll connect you with a lender I trust to see what's possible.

Do I need to stage?

Not always. Sometimes it's a few key pieces, sometimes it's rearranging what you already own. I'll tell you what will make a difference in your photos and showings, and what won't. If staging makes sense, I offer it myself, with my own furniture, whether your home is empty or you still live there.

Can I stay in my home while it's on the market?

Yes. Lots of my sellers do. We'll plan showings around your life, kids and pets included.

What about the online value estimates?

They're a starting point. Your updates, your street and this week's sales matter more. I'll give you a real price range with the sales behind it.
