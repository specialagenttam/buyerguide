Tam's take

Why I love Skylands

Skylands sits on the hills at the south end of Lake Oswego, near Glenmorrie and Mary's Landing. Part of the area sits outside city limits, in unincorporated Clackamas County.

Want acreage, privacy and big views? Start here. Some lots run three acres or more, and homes here hit the top of the Lake Oswego market. I hold eXp's Luxury certification, and Skylands homes deserve luxury-level marketing.

Good questions

Ask Tam

Is Skylands in Lake Oswego?

Partly. Some homes sit inside city limits and some sit in unincorporated Clackamas County. I'll confirm for every address.

Does a home here come with lake access?

Lake rights go with specific addresses, not whole neighborhoods. Oswego Lake is private. Access comes through the Lake Oswego Corporation, for lakefront homes and homes with a lake easement. I check the address on every home we tour.
