Tam's take

Why I love Westlake

Westlake sits north of Kruse Way on the western edge of Lake Oswego, close to the I-5 and Highway 217 interchange.

Westlake Park is my favorite park in town. Ball fields, courts and a covered playground make it busy all year.

The highlights

What you'll love

Westlake Park

Ball fields, tennis and basketball courts and a covered playground.

The Westlake Loop

A 2.8-mile walking route through the neighborhood.

Centerpointe

Shops and dining close to home.

Easy commutes

Quick drives to Portland, Beaverton and Hillsdale.

The homes

What you'll find

Single-family homes from the 1970s and newer, on curving streets.

  • Freeway access helps commuters. I'll check road noise on every home.
  • Some streets sit within a walk of Westlake Park.

Good questions

Ask Tam

How far is downtown Portland?

Westlake sits close to I-5 and Highway 217, so Portland, Beaverton and Hillsdale stay a quick drive away. Traffic varies, so I'll help you test the drive.

Does a home here come with lake access?

Lake rights go with specific addresses, not whole neighborhoods. Oswego Lake is private. Access comes through the Lake Oswego Corporation, for lakefront homes and homes with a lake easement. I check the address on every home we tour.
