Lake Oswego neighborhoods

Westridge

Hills, big trees and the Tualatin River.

Book a consultationSee Lake Oswego homes
Tam's take

Why I love Westridge

Westridge sits on the southwest side of Lake Oswego, south of Bryant, with hilly streets under tall evergreens.

Want a bigger home on a wooded lot with river trails close by? Start here. Many streets have no through traffic, and some homes catch views of the hills.

The highlights

What you'll love

River Run Park

Seven acres of green space along the Tualatin River.

Westridge Park

Two acres with a volleyball court, ball fields and a playground.

Wooded streets

Tall evergreens and firs on hilly roads.

Bryant Woods nearby

Boardwalks and a trail loop to the north.

The homes

What you'll find

Midcentury and contemporary homes from the 1960s and 1970s, plus custom brick New Traditional homes.

  • Median lots run close to a third of an acre.
  • Hilly lots mean views and steep driveways. I'll point out both.
Insider intel

Tam's Tips

  • Walk River Run Park on a fall afternoon.
  • Loop through Bryant Woods for the boardwalks.
  • Ask me which streets catch a view.
Good questions

Ask Tam

Is Westridge near the lake?

Oswego Lake sits about a mile north. Lake rights go with specific addresses, so I'll check each home.

Does a home here come with lake access?

Lake rights go with specific addresses, not whole neighborhoods. Oswego Lake is private. Access comes through the Lake Oswego Corporation, for lakefront homes and homes with a lake easement. I check the address on every home we tour.
