Tam's take

Why I love Forest Highlands

Forest Highlands sits on the north side of Lake Oswego, next to Tryon Creek State Natural Area. Many lots run about a third of an acre, and some run several acres.

Want privacy, trees and room for a big project? Start here. I'll help you size up lots, slopes and remodel potential.

The highlights

What you'll love

Tryon Creek

Tryon Creek State Natural Area and its trails sit on the northeast edge.

Woodmont Natural Park

A half-mile trail with overlooks.

Big lots

Room for gardens, shops, pools or privacy.

Easy errands

A quick drive to Oswego Towne Square and Boones Ferry Road.

The homes

What you'll find

Estate homes, split-levels, ranches and modern builds on large lots.

  • Large lots mean more upkeep. I'll help you weigh it.
  • Wooded lots mean tree rules. I'll check the city permits before you plan changes.
Insider intel

Tam's Tips

  • Hike Tryon Creek on a rainy day. The forest glows.
  • Walk Woodmont Natural Park for the overlooks.
  • Bring your contractor to the second showing. I'll set it up.
Good questions

Ask Tam

How big are the lots?

Many lots run about a third of an acre, and some run several acres. I'll pull the exact size for any home you like.

Does a home here come with lake access?

Lake rights go with specific addresses, not whole neighborhoods. Oswego Lake is private. Access comes through the Lake Oswego Corporation, for lakefront homes and homes with a lake easement. I check the address on every home we tour.

How do I see homes here?

Book a consultation with me first. I'll build a search for this neighborhood, send you new listings the day they hit and get you in the door.
