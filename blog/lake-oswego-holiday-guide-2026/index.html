Lake Oswego Holiday Guide 2026: Tree Lighting, Christmas Ships and Cozy Weekends

The rain moved in, the lights are going up and this town is about to sparkle. Here's my short list for the season, plus where to eat after.

I love Lake Oswego all year. But from mid-November to New Year's, this town turns into a snow globe without the snow. Lights on A Avenue, music in the plaza, boats glowing on the river.

Here's what's on my calendar, in order, to help you plan your weekends.

1. Holiday Marketplace: Nov. 17 to Dec. 24

The Arts Council of Lake Oswego fills its Artspace Gallery with gifts from local artisans. Jewelry, ceramics, prints, cards. If you hate the mall in December, start here.

Tam's tip: Go on a weekday afternoon. You'll have the place to yourself and time to chat with the people who made what you're buying.

2. Reunion Farmers' Market and wagon rides: Sat. Nov. 21

The farmers' market comes back to Millennium Plaza Park for one holiday Saturday, 9 am to 2 pm, with horse-drawn wagon rides. Grab your Thanksgiving pies and greens in one trip.

3. 62nd Annual Tree Lighting Festival: Fri. Nov. 27

The big one. The day after Thanksgiving, Millennium Plaza Park fills up from 3 to 7 pm, and the tree lights at 5:30. Bundle up and get there early if you want a spot near the tree.

Tam's tip: Park a few blocks away in First Addition or Evergreen and walk in. You'll skip the parking hunt and see the neighborhood lights on the way.

4. Shows at Lakewood Center for the Arts

Irving Berlin's White Christmas runs through Dec. 20 on the main stage. The Peppermint Bear & The Unhappy Princess plays the Side Door Stage Dec. 5 to 20. Book early. Holiday shows here sell out.

5. Tinseltown Trolley: Dec. 11 to 13

The Willamette Shore Trolley rolls out of its depot on State Street in holiday style. A sweet, old-school way to see the river.

6. Christmas Ships: Sat. Dec. 19

The Christmas Ships parade lights up the Willamette around 6 pm. Watch from George Rogers Park, Foothills Park or Roehr Park. Bring a thermos and a blanket. Trust me on the blanket.

Where to eat after

A few of my favorites:

  • Date night: Baird's on B.
  • Italian comfort food: Tucci's.
  • Coffee or a glass of wine: Lake Oswego Coffee House and Wine Bar. Find me there most Wednesdays.
  • Brunch the next morning: La Provence.

Buying or selling over the holidays?

Most people put their move on hold until January. Which is why the holidays work so well.

  • Buyers: fewer people out shopping means less competition and sellers ready to talk.
  • Sellers: fewer homes on the market means yours stands out, and the buyers out in December are serious.
  • Everyone: a home dressed for the holidays shows warm. Keep the decor simple and the lights on.
