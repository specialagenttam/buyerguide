Getting Ready to Sell: How to Prep Your Home for Top Dollar

The first two weeks on the market matter most. Here's how to make them count.

Buyers decide fast. Most of your best showings and strongest offers come in the first two weeks. Here's how to make sure your home is ready on day one.

1. Set your move-out date

Know where you're going next and when. It shapes your listing date and how flexible you can be with buyers.

2. Fix the small stuff

Drippy faucets, sticky doors and burned-out bulbs make buyers wonder what else was skipped. Knock them out.

3. Declutter and pre-pack

You're moving anyway. Pack early and let buyers see the space, not your stuff.

4. Paint, clean and refresh

Neutral paint, a deep clean and clean carpets give you one of the best returns on prep dollars.

5. Boost the curb appeal

Mow, edge, fresh bark dust and a sharp front door. The first photo is the front of your home, so make it count.

6. Consider a pre-listing inspection

For older homes, knowing what an inspector will find gives you time to fix it or price for it. Fewer surprises means smoother negotiations.

7. Price it right from the start

Overpricing is the most expensive mistake a seller makes. A home that sits gets stale, and buyers start asking what's wrong with it.
