Your Fall Home Checklist for the Pacific Northwest

Rain is coming. Here are the five fall jobs that protect your home and your wallet.

Fall in Lake Oswego is gorgeous. It's also when our homes start working overtime. A few hours now saves you from a soggy crawl space or a frozen pipe in January.

1. Clean the gutters after the leaves drop

Clogged gutters send water where it doesn't belong: your siding, your foundation and your crawl space. Clean them once most of the leaves are down and check that downspouts drain away from the house.

2. Blow out the sprinklers

Do it before the first hard freeze. A cracked line is a spring surprise nobody wants.

3. Disconnect hoses and cover outdoor faucets

Foam faucet covers cost a few dollars at the hardware store. A burst pipe costs a lot more.

4. Service the furnace and change the filter

A tune-up keeps it running efficiently and catches small problems before they turn into a no-heat call on the coldest night of the year.

5. Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors

Press the button on every one. Swap the batteries while you're up there.

Bonus: watch the moss

Our wet winters grow moss fast on roofs and walkways. Keep an eye on it now and plan a treatment in spring. Moss holds water against your roof and shortens its life.
