Get ready

Prep that pays off

Have a plan

Ask yourself if you're ready to move and if you know where you're going next. Your home could sell the day we list it, or it could take months. Check your finances for both, plus repairs that come up on the inspection report.

Make it sparkle

Deep clean, especially kitchens and baths. Recaulk, repaint, clean the carpets and clear out odors. Tidy cabinets, closets and the garage too, because buyers open everything.

Start packing now

Clutter makes rooms feel small and makes it harder for buyers to picture their own life there. Store extra furniture and personal photos. You get a head start on your move.

Let me run the vendors

With your OK, I coordinate projects, set start and finish dates and check the work. Need a name? Start with my trusted vendors.

Pricing

Comps matter. They don't tell the whole story.

  • We look at what sold nearby, what's sitting and why.
  • We count your home's best features, not just its square footage.
  • We pick a price range that pulls in the most qualified buyers.
  • Interest peaks when your home is new. We want the right price during those first weeks, so we don't have to correct it later.
Digital marketing

How I market your home online

Buyers start online, so your home needs to look great there and be easy to find. Here's what you get.

  • Your own web page. Photos, floor plan, video, the neighborhood and a way to request a tour. It lives on my website, not just on the portals.
  • Pro media. Photos, video, drone and 3D tours when they fit your home.
  • Everywhere buyers search. Your listing feeds out from the MLS to consumer sites.
  • Social campaigns. Reels and posts built for your home, shared with the vendors who helped prep it.
  • Comment-to-DM. Someone comments a keyword on a post and gets your listing page by message within seconds.
  • Video and email. Short videos, email to my network and search-friendly content that keeps your home visible.
  • Weekly numbers. Every Monday you see views, saves, showings and feedback.

Open houses

Open houses that work

A busy open house creates buzz and gets buyers moving. I promote it online and in the neighborhood, then follow up with everyone who comes through. Can't be there in person? Buyers from out of town can ask for a live video walkthrough.

Avoid these

Four mistakes that cost sellers

01

Running on emotion

It's okay if this is emotional. I'll bring the numbers when decisions need a clear head.

02

An unrealistic price

Buyers run comps too. Overpriced homes sit, and then they get discounted.

03

Skipping prep

A broken doorknob makes buyers wonder what else is wrong. Fresh eyes spot what you stopped seeing.

04

Hiding problems

Inspections find them anyway. Fix it, price for it or offer a credit. A pre-listing inspection helps you avoid surprises.

Words you'll hear

Plain-language terms

Appraisal

A professional's estimate of what the home is worth, based on the property and recent similar sales.

Closing costs

The fees paid when the property transfers, such as title, escrow, taxes and commissions.

Contingency

A contract term that lets terms change or end if a specific event happens by a set date.

Escrow

A neutral third party holds funds and documents until closing, typically a title company.

MLS

The database agents use for detailed listings of homes on the market.

Title

Ownership of the property, shown by a deed recorded with the county.
