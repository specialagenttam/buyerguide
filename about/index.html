The name

Why Special Agent?

Growing up, I wanted to be an FBI agent. Life had other plans, and real estate turned out to be my kind of mission. The name came to me one night in the hot tub with my husband, Casey, riffing on "Secret Agent Man." It stuck.

It fits how I work. I do the homework, I read the fine print and I go to bat for my clients. And I've always believed in standing out. Why fit in when you can stand out? That's why the neon.

Why I do this

I remember my first set of keys.

I bought my first home as a single parent. I still remember standing there with the keys in my hand, feeling like I'd climbed a mountain. Every late night, every budget spreadsheet, every "can we really do this?" had led to that moment.

That house isn't there anymore. It was torn down and a condo complex went up in its place. The building is gone, but the feeling of those first keys never left me.

That feeling is why I love this work. Getting to be a small part of someone else's big moment, whether it's a first home, a fresh start or a forever home, never gets old. I'm a sentimental one. Fair warning: I might tear up at your closing.

Where I come from

Home means a lot to my family.

My mom came to the United States when she was ten. Her family is Dutch Indonesian, and they started over in a new country and built a home from scratch. That makes me a first-generation American on her side, and it shaped how I see every front door I open for a client.

My dad used his GI Bill to buy land on the border of Oregon City and Mulino. Then he and many of my mom's "aunts" and "uncles" built my childhood home with their own hands. Every wall in that house came from people who loved us.

My ultimate dream? To buy that house back and put it in my family's name again. So when you tell me a house is more than a house, I get it.

She also taught me this: "When you're thinking about someone, reach out." So I do. Past clients hear from me on their home-iversary, their birthday and on random days when something reminds me of them. You don't stop being my client when we close. You become my people.

Before real estate, I spent years in financial services. That's where I learned to read the fine print, run the numbers and explain them in plain English. I got my license in March 2020, right as the world shut down. Timing has never been my strong suit. Persistence is.

Life outside the lockbox

Chaos, coffee, closings and cocktails.

I live in Mountain Park, where the trails are my therapy and the hills are my cardio. You'll find me at Wednesday coffee with the Lake Oswego Ladies Social or at a happy hour I probably planned.

At home, it's my husband Casey and our crew. My son Tristen is grown now. I spent his school years as a baseball mom, and these days I'm a cheer mom. And then there are the Frenchies: Yama, my heart dog, and Mojo, her baby girl. They run the house. I just pay the mortgage.

I'm also known for bold nails, a few tattoos and a laugh you can hear from the next room.
